What is NexAgent HMS and who is it designed for? ×

NexAgent HMS is a Hospital Management System designed for clinics, multi-specialty hospitals, and healthcare networks. It streamlines outpatient queueing, doctor consultation scheduling, bed occupancy tracking, and insurance TPA pre-authorizations under strict clinical safety guidelines.

How does NexAgent eliminate AI hallucination risks? +

NexAgent strictly separates unstructured language comprehension from database mutations. Model outputs are validated against deterministic business rules, spending caps, and clinical safety thresholds. Actions exceeding risk parameters mandate human-in-the-loop approvals before execution.

What is the relationship between NexAgent HMS and other vertical solutions? +

NexAgent HMS is currently our primary active product focus in active development and pilot testing. Solutions for other industries (Hospitality, B2B, Retail, Services, Fintech) represent workflow automation concepts and blueprints currently in development.

How is customer and patient data protected? +

NexAgent is architected with security-first engineering: data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), access is governed by strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and customer data is never used to train public models. We support regional data residency in India and international cloud locations.
