Do I need to train the AI myself? ×

No, NexAgent learns from your existing knowledge base, past tickets, and CRM data. It gets smarter over time based on agent corrections and resolved outcomes.

What integrations do you support? +

We support all major customer service tools including Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Jira, Slack, and custom webhooks/REST APIs.

Can I control what NexAgent automates? +

Yes. You can enforce granular rule guardrails, set strict confidence thresholds, and mandate human-in-the-loop approvals for sensitive operations like refunds or account closures.

Is my data safe? +

NexAgent is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and ISO 27001 audited. Your customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and never used to train public LLMs.

How long does setup take? +

Standard setup takes less than 15 minutes with our one-click OAuth integrations. Custom enterprise pipelines usually deploy within 2-3 business days.

What does NexAgent actually do? +

NexAgent reads incoming support tickets, understands customer intent, retrieves related records from your CRM, checks policies, and either resolves the issue autonomously or drafts actions for your approval.
