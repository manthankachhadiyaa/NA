What makes NexAgent different from typical chatbots and deflection tools? ×

NexAgent is not a conversational chatbot; it is an autonomous operational execution layer. While chatbots only chat with users, NexAgent reads, analyzes, and executes verified mutations directly in your CRM, helpdesk, or hospital management database under strict policy rules.

How does NexAgent eliminate AI hallucination and prevent unauthorized actions? +

All automated actions are governed by our deterministic Policy Governor. Model responses are grounded exclusively in your verified knowledge base and CRM data. Actions exceeding defined financial or clinical thresholds require mandatory human-in-the-loop approval before executing.

What is NexAgent's current focus between Enterprise Ops and Healthcare HMS? +

Both products are powered by our core deterministic engine. NexAgent Operations Layer is in full commercial production for enterprise support and CRM workflows. NexAgent Healthcare & HMS is our major vertical focus, addressing hospital queue bottlenecks, OPD/IPD flows, and insurance pre-authorization validation.

What is the roadmap status for Plexus / Imuna AI? +

Plexus / Imuna AI is our next-generation high-dimensional clinical intelligence context engine. It is currently under active development by our engineering team, with private enterprise beta deployments launching in Q4 2026.

Where is client data processed, and is NexAgent compliant with security standards? +

NexAgent is HIPAA-ready, SOC 2 Type II compliant, and ISO 27001 audited. Client data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We support data residency in India and international cloud regions, ensuring complete compliance with global privacy regulations.

How long does an enterprise deployment typically take? +

Standard helpdesk and CRM connections deploy in under 30 minutes via certified OAuth connectors. Custom enterprise pipelines and Hospital Information System (HIS) integrations typically take 2-3 weeks including sandbox policy testing and verification.
